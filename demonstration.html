THE REAL PROJECT WORKFLOW

What happens behind the scenes?

The physical VOCULUS prototype follows these stages when processing a supported spoken calculation.

01

Speak

A mathematical instruction is spoken into a microphone.

02

Recognize

Speech recognition converts speech into text.

03

Calculate

Python processes the instruction and calculates the answer.

04

Transmit

The result is sent to Arduino using serial communication.

05

Display

Fourteen servo motors position the physical display segments.

Important demonstration note: The calculator above is a browser-based arithmetic simulator. Its inputs are entered manually. The soundwave is decorative. Neither feature accesses a microphone, connects to the Arduino or moves the physical servo motors. The actual project uses its separate Python, speech-recognition and hardware system.
