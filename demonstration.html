What Happens Behind the Scenes?

Four stages turn a spoken calculation into a physical answer.

STEP 01

Voice Input

The microphone captures the spoken mathematical expression.

STEP 02

Python Processing

Python recognises the expression and calculates the answer.

STEP 03

Arduino Control

The result is sent to the Arduino Mega through serial communication.

STEP 04

Physical Output

Fourteen servo motors position the segments to display the answer.
