More than a calculator.

A combination of software, electronics and mechanical movement — bringing a spoken calculation to life.

🎙

Voice Input

Spoken mathematical instructions provide the input instead of a conventional keypad.

⌘

Python Processing

Python handles the recognized instruction and performs the arithmetic calculation.

⚡

Arduino Control

Serial communication transfers the calculated result to the Arduino controller.

⚙

Servo Mechanism

Fourteen servo motors position the physical segments to form two digits.

◈

Physical Display

A mechanical seven-segment-style display makes the calculated result visible.

✦

Hands-Free Concept

UNDER THE HOOD

From speech to digits.

Every calculation travels through a sequence of software and hardware stages.

01

Voice Input

A spoken mathematical instruction enters the system through a microphone.

02

Recognition

Speech recognition converts the spoken instruction into text.

03

Calculation

Python processes the instruction and calculates the answer.

04

Communication

The result is transmitted to the Arduino through serial communication.

05

Display

Servo motors move the segments to display the result.
