THE STORY BEHIND THE INNOVATION

Every Idea Has
A Journey.

VOCULUS is more than a calculator. It is a journey of imagination, experimentation, problem-solving and teamwork. Here's how our idea takes shape.

The VOCULUS Journey

From the first concept to a working prototype.

💡
STAGE 01 · THE IDEA

A Different Kind of Calculator

We imagined a calculator that could understand spoken mathematical expressions instead of depending on a keypad. The goal was to make calculation more interactive.

🧠
STAGE 02 · PLANNING

Connecting the Concepts

We planned a system combining a microphone, Python, an Arduino Mega and servo motors. Each part would have a specific role in turning speech into a displayed answer.

💻
STAGE 03 · PROGRAMMING

Giving the Idea a Brain

We worked on Python programming for speech recognition and arithmetic processing, along with Arduino code to control the servo positions.

⚙️
STAGE 04 · BUILDING

Turning Code into Movement

We worked on the physical display using fourteen servo motors. Seven segments form each digit, allowing the result to appear as a two-digit number.

🔌
STAGE 05 · INTEGRATION

Making Everything Communicate

The Python program and Arduino Mega are connected through serial communication. We tested the electronics and worked on coordinating the servo movements.

🚀
STAGE 06 · PRESENTATION

Ready to Demonstrate

The final goal is to demonstrate how a spoken calculation can travel through software and electronics to become a visible physical answer.
