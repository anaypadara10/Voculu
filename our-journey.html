FROM AN IDEA TO REALITY

Every great idea
has a journey.

VOCULUS wasn't built in a single day. It took planning, experimenting, coding, testing and teamwork to bring a voice-powered calculator to life.

The Making of VOCULUS

Every milestone brought us one step closer.

💡
01 — THE IDEA

A Different Kind of Calculator

We imagined a calculator that could understand spoken mathematical instructions instead of relying on a keypad.

📐
02 — PLANNING

Turning Ideas Into a Design

We planned the physical display, electronic components and software needed to make the concept work.

💻
03 — CODING

Building the Software

Python was selected to process mathematical instructions, perform calculations and communicate results to Arduino.

⚙️
04 — ELECTRONICS

Making the Display Move

We worked with Arduino and servo motors to create a physical display made from moving seven-segment parts.

🧪
05 — TESTING

Testing the Components

We tested servo movement and Arduino communication, checking that the display positions could be updated.

🚀
06 — THE PROTOTYPE

Bringing VOCULUS Together

Software, electronics and the physical structure come together in our voice-powered calculator prototype.
